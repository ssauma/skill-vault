# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, reviews, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct violates this standard. Reports are reviewed privately and handled according to context and impact.

Report conduct concerns through the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.
